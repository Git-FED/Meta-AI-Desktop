DOCUMENT / PRIVACY

Privacy, plainly.

This static site does not operate a custom account database. Payment and newsletter forms route to the named third-party provider only when you choose to continue.

Email-list authentication

The Join / Login page uses Supabase Auth to create and manage email-list accounts, sign-in sessions, and password-reset messages. The Supabase project receives the credentials and account data submitted through that page under its own service terms. Do not use the email-list form if you do not want to create an account.

What this package uses

The age gate stores a simple confirmation flag in your browser's local storage. The site may load fonts from Google Fonts and the Discord widget from Discord when those pages are opened. Payment providers apply their own privacy policies.

For questions, return to the support hub or the home page.
